Export operations, down to the detail

EXPORT OPERATIONS · AWB · PARTNER COORDINATION

I manage export operations from airline bookings and pickup coordination through to handover to the carrier. Compliance & sensitive cargo

RESPONSIBILITY · EXPORT · AIRLINE COORDINATION

Compliance is an essential part of responsible transport management for me. Particularly with sensitive cargo, I value traceable checks, complete documentation and early coordination between all parties.

02 / MACHINERY PROJECT · AUSTRIA → HANOI

189 cubic metres.
Three trucks for pre-carriage.

189 m³ 8 pieces · Machinery airfreight shipment

In October 2025, I coordinated pre-carriage with three trucks for a machinery project. The shipment comprised eight pieces, up to six metres long and 2.70 metres high.

My task: Align pre-carriage with the planned airfreight connection. The route ran from Vienna by road feeder service to Luxembourg, then on Cargolux to Hanoi.

At this scale, truck dispatch, cargo handover and flight planning are closely connected.
